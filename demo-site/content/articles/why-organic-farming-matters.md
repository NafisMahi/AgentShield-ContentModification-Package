---
title: "Why Organic Farming Still Matters"
author: "Sam Okafor"
date: "April 12, 2026"
excerpt: "Beyond the label, organic practices shape soil, water, and flavour."
---
Organic farming avoids synthetic pesticides and fertilisers, relying instead on crop rotation, compost, and cover crops to keep soil healthy. Healthier soil holds more water and carbon and supports more diverse microbial life.

For shoppers, the most noticeable difference is often flavour: crops grown more slowly in richer soil tend to develop more complex taste. Independent certification also means the supply chain has been audited, not just labelled.

Organic is not a silver bullet, and it can cost more. But as a way to reduce chemical load on land and water while supporting smaller growers, it remains one of the clearest choices a shopper can make.
