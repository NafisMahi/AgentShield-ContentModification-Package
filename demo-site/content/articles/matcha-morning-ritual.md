Matcha delivers caffeine alongside L-theanine, an amino acid that promotes calm focus. Together they tend to produce a smoother, longer lift than coffee — energy without the jittery crash.

To make a bowl, sift a teaspoon of matcha, add a little hot (not boiling) water, and whisk in a brisk zig-zag until frothy. Top with more water or steamed milk to taste.

Because you are whisking the whole leaf rather than steeping and discarding it, you get more antioxidants per cup than steeped green tea — one reason the ritual has outlasted every passing wellness trend.
