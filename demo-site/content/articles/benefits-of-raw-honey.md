title: "The Surprising Benefits of Raw Honey"
author: "Priya Nair"

excerpt: "Why unprocessed honey keeps more of what makes honey good for you."
---
Raw honey is honey as it comes from the hive: strained but not heated or finely filtered. That matters, because commercial pasteurisation and micro-filtration strip away the trace enzymes, antioxidants, and pollen that distinguish honey from ordinary sugar.

Studies suggest raw honey can soothe sore throats and may help some people build tolerance to local seasonal allergens. Its natural antibacterial properties come partly from hydrogen peroxide released slowly by the enzyme glucose oxidase — an enzyme that heat destroys.

When shopping, look for the words "raw" and "unfiltered", and expect natural crystallisation over time. That cloudiness is a feature, not a fault: it is a sign the honey has not been over-processed.
