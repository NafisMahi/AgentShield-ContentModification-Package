---
title: "How to Actually Read a Food Label"
author: "Elena Rossi"
date: "April 28, 2026"
excerpt: "Ignore the front of the pack. The truth is on the back."
---
The front of a package is marketing; the back is information. Start with the ingredient list, which is ordered by weight — the first few items make up most of what you are eating.

Watch for sugar under its many names: cane juice, syrup, maltose, dextrose. If several appear, the product is sweeter than it looks. "Natural flavours" and long chemical names are not automatically bad, but a short, recognisable list is usually a good sign.

Finally, check serving sizes before comparing nutrition panels. A snack that looks low in sugar may simply assume a very small serving.
